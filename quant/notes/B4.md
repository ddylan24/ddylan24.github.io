# B4 — Fat tails
CRSP market daily log returns 1926-07 → 2026-08 (n=26,317; Ken French): skew −0.46, kurtosis 20.0 (normal 3).
Exceedances observed vs normal: >3σ 463 vs 71; >4σ 192 vs 1.7; >5σ 104 vs 0.015; >7σ 32 vs 7e−8; >10σ 5 vs 4e−19. Worst: 1987-10-19, −17.4% = −17.8σ (normal p ~ 6e−71).
Student-t fit ν=2.49 — overcorrects at the extreme (predicts 27 days >10σ vs 5 observed): unconditional tails are a MIXTURE of calm and turbulent volatility regimes, not one t distribution.
Hill tail index α of losses: 3.24 (top 100), 3.04 (250), 2.83 (500) ⇒ power-law tails with α≈3 ("inverse cubic law", Gopikrishnan et al. 1998/Gabaix): variance finite, kurtosis (4th moment) theoretically infinite → sample kurtosis is unstable and dominated by a few days.
Aggregational Gaussianity: kurtosis 20 (1d) → 8.9 (5d) → 8.2 (21d) → 7.0 (63d); skew gets MORE negative with horizon (−0.46 → −0.84).
99% 1-day VaR backtest 1991–2026, rolling 1y window, n=8,983, expected 90 breaches: normal 216 (2.4×), historical 146, Student-t(4) 143. Kupiec POF LR: 128.5, 29.8, 26.9 — all reject (>3.84). Even historical VaR fails because a 1-year window reacts slowly to volatility clustering → conditional vol (GARCH/EWMA) needed (C2).
Kupiec LR = −2[(N−x)ln(1−p) + x ln p − (N−x)ln(1−x/N) − x ln(x/N)] ~ χ²(1).
