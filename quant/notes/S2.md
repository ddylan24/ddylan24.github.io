# S2: Kalshi hourly BTC markets. Calibration by time to expiry

**Question.** R1 hinted that Kalshi's daily BTC contracts are *under-confident* 6 hours out (logit slope 1.35). Does that hold across horizons in the hourly events, and does selling cheap contracts pay?

## Data (`code/s2_fetch.py`, `code/s2_horizon.py`)
- 400 settled KXBTCD events of every hour (Sep 27–Oct 14, about 17 days): 26,151 "above K" markets within ±4% of spot at T−12h.
- Hourly bid/ask via batch candles. Perp prices are re-stamped by bar close (R1 lesson).
- Quotes kept at T−1/2/3/6/12h if the spread is ≤10¢ and the mid is 2–98¢. Only about 430 per horizon qualify: most strikes are deep in or out of the money, or quoted wide.

## Results (day-clustered)
| horizon | n | slope | se | 5–20¢: priced → won | 80–95¢: priced → won |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1h | 83 | 1.50 | 0.42 | 0.110 → 0.000 | 0.886 → 0.933 |
| 2h | 117 | 1.24 | 0.24 | 0.113 → 0.105 | 0.895 → 0.958 |
| 3h | 143 | 1.08 | 0.15 | 0.117 → 0.250 | 0.898 → 0.966 |
| 6h | 215 | 1.29 | 0.19 | 0.114 → 0.051 | 0.888 → 0.897 |
| 12h | 250 | 1.39 | 0.25 | 0.108 → 0.053 | 0.892 → 0.894 |
| **pooled** | 619 | **1.15** | 0.14 | 0.114 → 0.105 | |

- Every point estimate of the slope is above 1, which leans under-confident, but pooled it's **1.15 ± 0.14, not significant**.
- Selling 5–20¢ contracts at the bid after fees: **−0.3¢ (t −0.1)** pooled. Per-horizon "t-stats" like +9.6 at 1h come from 15 trades on a handful of days. With so few clusters the cluster SE breaks down, so ignore them.
- **Conclusion:** no tradable short-horizon miscalibration in 17 days of hourly BTC markets. The R1 hint (slope 1.35 at 6h over 68 days) stays a hint.
- Kalshi's settled history reaches back only weeks, so a real test needs *continuous collection*. That's a task for the user's home machine (CONTEXT §5b).

## Lessons
1. Cluster-robust t-stats need many clusters. With fewer than ~20 days and a handful of trades, they're meaningless.
2. Weak, consistent-sign estimates (all slopes > 1) are a reason to collect more data, not to trade.
