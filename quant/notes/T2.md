# T2: Market making a binary with the measured volatility law

**Setup (`code/t2_mm_binary.py`).**
- 400 simulated 30-day binaries with 5-minute steps.
- Fair value diffuses by the S9 law (daily sd of p = 0.94·φ(N⁻¹p)/τ^0.53), plus S10-style jumps (0.004/day, 20–50¢).
- Uninformed takers hit each side with probability A·e^{−kδ} per step (A = 0.6, k = 120 per $, so δ = 1¢ gives 0.18). 100 contracts per fill.
- At a jump, the stale quote on the wrong side is picked off. P&L is settled at resolution, y ~ Bernoulli(p_T).
- Arrival parameters are illustrative, not fitted, so compare strategies rather than reading off absolute $.

## Strategies
- (a) fixed 1¢ half-spread around fair value
- (b) half-spread = 3× local per-step sd (S9 law), floor 0.5¢
- (c) (b) plus an Avellaneda–Stoikov-style inventory skew: reservation price r = p − (inventory/Q)·2σ_step

## Results ($ per market)
| strategy | jumps | mean | sd | mean/sd | 5% worst | picked off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fixed 1¢ | no / yes | 9.7 / 11.1 | 158 / 157 | 0.06 / 0.07 | −244 / −260 | 0 / 4.4 |
| vol-scaled | no / yes | 10.8 / 4.5 | 135 / 130 | 0.08 / 0.03 | −186 / −205 | 0 / 4.3 |
| **vol + inventory skew** | no / yes | 8.3 / 7.5 | **43 / 43** | **0.19 / 0.17** | **−55 / −55** | 0 / 4.3 |

- **Inventory control dominates.** Skewing quotes by inventory cuts P&L sd 3.6× and the 5% tail 4.5×, for a small cost in mean. Without it, the book's random inventory at resolution (a 0/1 payoff) swamps the spread income. This matches the binary market-making result in N3 (sd ~±12 contracts, set by inventory at resolution).
- **Vol-scaled spreads help modestly** (sd −15%). They widen near 50% and late in life, where S9 says moves are largest.
- **Jumps** cost about $4 per market in pickoffs, roughly 40–90% of the mean edge in these settings. In practice, pull or widen quotes ahead of scheduled news (Q2: Fed decisions move 90% in 6 seconds).

## Lessons
1. For a binary maker: control inventory first, scale the spread by φ(N⁻¹p)/τ^{~0.5} second, and handle news and jumps third.
2. Edge per market is small next to inventory risk, so diversify across many uncorrelated markets. With mean/sd ≈ 0.19 per market, 100 independent markets give about 1.9 for the book.
