# E3 — Kelly derivation & drawdown math
Discrete: bet fraction f, win prob p pays b, lose q: G(f)=p ln(1+fb)+q ln(1−f); G'(f)=0 ⇒ f* = p − q/b. p=.55, b=1 → f*=0.10, growth 0.005/bet.
Continuous: g(L)=r + L(μ−r) − ½L²σ² (Itô) ⇒ L* = (μ−r)/σ²; g(L*) = r + ½SR². Multi-asset: w* = Σ⁻¹(μ−r) = tangency portfolio scaled — Kelly ≡ mean-variance with risk aversion 1.
Drawdown law (GBM, fraction c of Kelly): P(wealth ever falls to x of start) = x^(2/c − 1). Full Kelly: P(halve)=50%, P(lose 75%)=25%. Half Kelly: x³ → 12.5%, 1.6%. 2× Kelly: certain (eventually).
