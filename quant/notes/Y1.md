# Y1: Are makers profitable in Kalshi 15-minute markets? (7 days, marked to settlement)

**Why.** X1 found 15-minute FX makers *earning* +1.2 to +3.3¢ per contract (5-minute markouts, one 24-hour window). Here I test that over a week, marked to the real economic outcome: **settlement**.

**Data (`code/y1_fx15_robust.py`).**
- Every settled market in the last 7 days for KXEURUSD15M, KXUSDJPY15M, KXAUDUSD15M (≈470 each, one up/down market per 15 minutes vs the opening target) and the crypto controls KXBTC15M and KXETH15M (≈665 each). All public fills: **6.7M fills**.
- Maker P&L per contract to settlement: taker bought YES at P → P − y; taker bought NO → y − P.
- Clustered by market.

## Results
| series | contracts | maker P&L to settlement | per contract | t (market-clustered) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EUR/USD 15m | 2.54M | +$2.2k | **+0.09¢** | 0.2 |
| USD/JPY 15m | 2.08M | +$21.3k | +1.03¢ | 1.0 |
| AUD/USD 15m | 0.83M | −$3.0k | −0.36¢ | −0.7 |
| BTC 15m | 586M | −$40k | −0.01¢ | −0.5 |
| ETH 15m | 110M | +$237k | +0.22¢ | 2.2 |

- **The X1 "FX makers earn 1–3¢" result does not survive a week to settlement.** Maker fill P&L is statistically zero in all three FX series. The by-hour and by-price patterns flip sign from bucket to bucket, which is noise.
- **What it means for LIP:** in short programs, fills roughly *break even*, so the reward pool is close to pure profit for whoever earns the share. That's better than X2's long programs (rewards ≈ 1.5× fill losses), but a smaller edge than X1 implied.
- The crypto 15-minute books are enormous (586M BTC contracts in a week) and makers break even there too.
- **Lessons:**
  1. One 24-hour window and a 5-minute markout overstated the effect. Mark to settlement, cluster by market, and use a week or more.
  2. Corrected X1 accordingly.
