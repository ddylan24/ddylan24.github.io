# N2 — Kalshi calibration: daily high-temperature markets (6 cities)
API limits: settled-market listing only reaches back ~2 months (Aug–Oct 2026); general settled list is dominated by same-day sports/combos (mve_filter=exclude helps). Candlesticks: /series/{S}/markets/{T}/candlesticks?start_ts&end_ts&period_interval=60 (yes_bid/yes_ask close_dollars).
Series KXHIGHNY, KXHIGHCHI, KXHIGHMIA, KXHIGHLAX, KXHIGHAUS, KXHIGHDEN; settled 2026-08-08 → 10-14: 2,448 bucket markets in 408 city-days. Fixed schedule (close at local midnight) → price 24h before close is pre-day forecast; mid of bid/ask, spread ≤10¢: n=2,188.
Brier .110 vs base .152 (skill 27%). Calibration slope b 1.18 ± .07 (>1 ⇒ classic favorite–longshot: longshots overpriced). Mid-priced (5–95¢) outcome − price −.003 (t −1.0) → well calibrated in the middle.
Bins: <5¢ avg price .023 won .009; 5–15¢ .092 → .073; 15–30¢ .220 → .208; 30–50¢ .389 → .408; 50–70¢ .574 → .576.
Sum of bucket mids per city-day median 1.040 (≈4% overround — buyers of the full set overpay).
Selling cheap buckets at the BID (= buying NO at 1−bid), Kalshi taker fee 0.07·P·(1−P) per contract (order-level rounding): <5¢ buckets avg bid 1.75¢, YES rate 0.89%, fee .12¢ → EV +0.74¢/contract (clustered t 1.18, n 783, 7 losses of ~98¢); 5–15¢ → +0.56¢. Suggestive, not significant (2 months); classic picking-up-pennies tail risk.
