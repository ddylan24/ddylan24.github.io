# U2: Where does BTC price discovery happen? Kalshi vs Polymarket response to spot

**Plan vs reality.** I meant to test lead-lag on identical contracts. But Polymarket's *hourly* BTC threshold markets ("Bitcoin above ___ on Oct 14, 7PM ET", 20 strikes) traded **about $15 per strike**, so they're dead. Instead I measured how fast each venue's *daily* contracts respond to spot BTC, minute by minute.

## Data (`code/u2_response.py`; last 6 hours to 2026-10-14 23:46 UTC)
- Spot: Deribit BTC-PERPETUAL 1-minute closes, re-stamped to bar end (R1 lesson). 1-minute sd 3.9bp.
- Kalshi: KXBTCD Oct 15 5pm-ET contracts, the 8 strikes nearest spot. 1-minute bid/ask candles via the batch endpoint (period_interval=1). Mids.
- Polymarket: "Bitcoin above X on October 15" (noon ET) strikes within 2%. Taker trades, YES-equivalent price, last per minute, forward-filled. Only two strikes traded at all: 48 trades / $2.2k and 30 trades / $1.4k in 6 hours.
- Regression of Δp_t (¢) on spot returns at lags 0–5 (bp), pooled over strikes, HAC SEs.

## Results
| venue | ¢ per 1bp, lags 0…5 | total | share at lag 0 | by lag 1 | R² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kalshi quotes | +0.120, +0.033, +0.026, +0.004, +0.004, +0.004 | **0.191** | **63%** | 80% | **0.42** |
| Polymarket trades | +0.009, +0.009, +0.005, +0.009, +0.010, 0.000 | 0.041 | 21% | 43% | 0.02 |

- **Kalshi is where price discovery happens.** Its quotes absorb most of a spot move within the same minute and almost all within two.
  - The total response of 0.19¢ per bp matches theory. Digital delta ≈ φ(d₂)/(σ√T)·10⁻⁴ ≈ 0.4/(0.37·√(0.88/365))·10⁻⁴ ≈ **0.22¢ per bp** for an at-the-money one-day contract.
- **Polymarket's BTC threshold markets are thin and stale.** Trade prices barely track spot. Q3 found their *quotes* sat close to options-implied values, which means market makers keep resting orders roughly right, but trading is sparse.
- Implications:
  - For live BTC-binary work (e.g., the U4 candidate), Kalshi is the venue with real flow.
  - On Polymarket, stale resting quotes after a sharp spot move are a classic pick-off target. The cost of trying is crossing about a 1¢ spread on small size.

## Lessons
1. Check volume per contract before designing a strategy. Many listed markets are effectively empty.
2. A venue's response coefficient should match the contract's theoretical delta. If it doesn't, either the data is stale or there's an opportunity.
