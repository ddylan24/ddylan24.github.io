# G3 — Triple-barrier labels + meta-labeling (SPY, real data)
Primary: CUSUM(k=2, σ20) events, bet on reversal (long after down-move, short after up-move). Triple barrier ±1.5σ√10, vertical 10 days. 1,422 events; hit 48.1%; mean +0.13%/trade. 92% of label windows overlap the next event.
Features at event time: vol ratio (σ20/avg σ20 prior 60d), distance to 200d SMA, 5d & 20d return, VIX level (FRED), 5d VIX change, side, weekday. Model: random forest (300 trees, min leaf 20).
CV AUC: shuffled K-fold .597 vs purged+embargoed (14d) sequential .593 → leakage negligible HERE (features not overlapping-label-derived); can be large elsewhere (AFML ch.7) — test, don't assume.
Walk-forward 2008–2026 (train only on events whose labels ended before test year): OOS AUC .603.
- take every event: 994 trades, hit 47.0%, mean +.09%, per-trade SR .027
- meta P>.5: 475 trades, hit 56.0%, mean +.50%, SR .129 (5× better)
- BUT 74% of meta-selected trades are longs (vs 42% overall). Simple rule "longs only" (buy-the-dip): 416 trades, hit 61.1%, mean +.57%, SR .146 — beats the ML filter.
- Within longs, meta still sorts: top-half P mean +.90% vs bottom-half +.25% (hit ~same) → some value for SIZING.
- Feature importance: ret5 .18, vix_chg .15, ret20 .15, dist200 .14, vix .13, vol_ratio .13, side .10, dow .04.
Lesson: always benchmark ML against the simplest rule it might be rediscovering; here the forest mostly learned "don't short an index that drifts up".
