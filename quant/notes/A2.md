# A2 — Statistics of the Sharpe ratio
SR per period = μ/σ. Estimator SR̂ = x̄/s.
- Lo (2002), iid normal: Var(SR̂) ≈ (1 + SR²/2)/T.
- Mertens (2002), iid non-normal: Var(SR̂) ≈ (1 + SR²/2 − γ3·SR + (γ4−3)/4·SR²)/T   (γ3 skew, γ4 kurtosis, non-excess).
- Annualized: SE(SR_ann) ≈ √(q/T)·√(1+…) where q=periods/yr. Since T/q = years ⇒ SE(SR_ann) ≈ 1/√years for small per-period SR. **Sampling more often does not reduce Sharpe uncertainty; only more calendar time does.**
- PSR(SR*) = Φ[(SR̂ − SR*)·√(T−1) / √(1 − γ3·SR̂ + (γ4−1)/4·SR̂²)]  (Bailey & López de Prado 2012).
- MinTRL = 1 + (1 − γ3·SR̂ + (γ4−1)/4·SR̂²)·(z_α/(SR̂ − SR*))²  observations.
- Lo autocorrelation: SR(q) = η(q)·SR, η(q)= q/√(q + 2Σ_{k=1}^{q−1}(q−k)ρ_k). Positive autocorr (smoothing, stale prices) inflates naive √q annualization.
Verification (simulation, T=1260 daily): normal → simulated SE 0.0282 = formula 0.0282. Fat/skewed daily (skew −0.22, kurt 13) → 0.0281 vs Mertens 0.0284: at DAILY frequency non-normality barely matters because SR per day is tiny (SR² terms ≈ 0).
Non-normality matters at low frequency/high SR: monthly SR 0.5 (≈1.7 ann) with skew −3, kurt 20 → SE ×1.81 vs normal.
SPY real: 1999–2026 annual SR 0.44 ± 0.19 (skew −0.20, kurt 14.3), PSR(SR>0)=0.989, MinTRL 14.3 yrs. 2017–2026: SR 0.79 ± 0.33.
MinTRL (95% one-sided, daily, normal): SR 0.5 → 10.8 yrs; 1.0 → 2.7 yrs; 2.0 → 0.7 yrs.
Lo adj: SPY monthly lag-1 ρ≈0 → 0.63 vs 0.60. Smoothed series ρ=0.5: naive 1.79 vs adjusted 1.31.
