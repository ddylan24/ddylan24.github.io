# P3 — LMSR market maker (Hanson 2003/2007)
Cost function C(q) = b·ln Σ_i e^{q_i/b}; price p_i = e^{q_i/b}/Σ e^{q_j/b} (softmax); trade cost = C(q') − C(q). Prices always sum to 1 (no Dutch book).
Worst-case MM loss = b·ln n (binary b=1000 → $693; n=10 → $2,303; n=128 → $4,852). b = liquidity: shares to move binary p0→p1: Δq = b·ln[p1(1−p0)/(p0(1−p1))] (50→80%: 1,386 shares for $916.29).
Informed trader who moves price from 0.5 to true p: MM expected loss = b·KL(p‖0.5) — verified exactly (p=.6 $20.14; .8 $192.74; .95 $494.63; .999 $685.24). The MM is a paid subsidy for information; b trades depth vs max subsidy.
Polymarket today uses a CLOB (makers quote; Avellaneda–Stoikov-style inventory logic applies, Module 11) plus liquidity rewards; LMSR used by earlier/other venues (e.g., Augur-era, Manifold-like AMMs).
# P4 — Arbitrage (live Polymarket snapshot 2026-10-14, gamma events API, bestBid/bestAsk)
Mechanics (Polymarket docs, neg-risk): in a multi-outcome event a NO in outcome A converts to 1 YES in every other outcome (NegRiskAdapter). Holding all n NOs: convert one, merge YES+NO pairs ⇒ n−1 USDC immediately. So:
- Buy-all-YES: pay Σask, receive $1 at resolution ⇒ profit only if Σask < e^{−rT} (time value!), capital locked.
- Buy-all-NO: pay n − Σbid, convert/merge to n−1 now ⇒ instant profit Σbid − 1 (before fees/gas).
Snapshot: 2,100 active events; 632 negRisk events with quotes on every outcome. Σmid median .995 (5–95%: .932–1.105).
Σask<1 in 116 events (median gap .9¢) — mostly NOT arbitrage: incomplete outcome sets after filtering/placeholder "Other", and for long-dated events the gap is below T-bill carry (2028 party market Σask .990 → 0.5%/yr vs 4.25% T-bills; needs Σask < e^{−.0425·2.07}=.916).
Σbid>1 in 15 events (median .8¢, max 1.5¢), all fee-enabled; e.g., NBA 2027 champion Σbid 1.010 (n=30 books to hit), Fed December decision 1.008, World Series 1.002. Edges ≈ fees + spread on the thin side; best-quote sizes small.
Literature: Saguillo, Ghafouri, Kiffer & Suarez-Tangil (2025), arXiv:2508.03474 — "market rebalancing" + "combinatorial" arbitrage on Polymarket; LLMs to detect logically dependent markets; ≈$40M realized arbitrage profit over their window. → arbitrage exists but is competed by bots; what's left at the touch is ~1¢ and fee-sized.
