# S3: When to exit a binary early, and the "high-probability bond" trade

## Exit math
- You hold YES. Your belief is q, the market bid is p − s (half-spread s), the T-bill rate is r and time left is τ.
- Selling now gets p − s. Holding is worth q·e^{−rτ} in present value. **Sell iff p − s > q·e^{−rτ}.**
- If you agree with the market (q = p), sell when s < p(1 − e^{−rτ}). The break-even horizon is τ* = −ln(1 − s/p)/r.
  - With a 1¢ half-spread and r = 4.25%: **p = 0.95 → 91 days**, p = 0.50 → 174 days.
  - Any agreed-upon position with more time left than that should be sold and the cash put in T-bills. A 99¢ contract resolving in a year is worth only 94.9¢ held.
- Corollary (P34): long-dated near-certain contracts *must* trade below their probability. Buyers who ignore time value are paying for that.

## "Favorites as a bond" on Polymarket (`code/s3_favorites.py`)
- A popular retail idea: buy contracts at 95¢+ and "earn 5% safely."
- Test on the 1,625 resolved markets with daily prices (P2):
  - Enter on the first day either side reaches the threshold, paying price + 0.5¢.
  - Hold to resolution. Exclude the last day before close (look-ahead).
  - Compare with T-bills over the same holding period. Event-level t-stats.

| entry threshold | n | win rate | avg price paid | median hold | excess vs T-bill | t | annualized |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≥0.90 | 1,476 | 0.925 | 0.958 | 105d | **−4.9%** | −4.9 | −10.5%/yr |
| ≥0.95 | 1,340 | 0.960 | 0.978 | 101d | **−3.2%** | −3.7 | −5.6%/yr |
| ≥0.97 | 1,273 | 0.973 | 0.987 | 88d | **−2.8%** | −3.8 | −4.9%/yr |

- Negative in every year (2024, 2025, 2026). Favorites at 95¢+ won only 96% of the time, and a 4% loss rate at −100% each wipes out the 2–3¢ of carry.
- It's the mirror image of P2's finding that YES longshots are underpriced and of R6's excess movement. Prices that first touch 95¢ have overshot.
- Caveats:
  - Daily last/mid prices, so the real ask is worse.
  - First touch only.
  - Selection on the top ~2,100 markets by volume.
  - The reverse may still not be tradable: buying the 3–5¢ side pays a large relative spread and ties up capital for about 100 days.

## Lessons
1. Picking up nickels at 95¢ is negative-expectation on Polymarket: you're short a tail that happens more often than priced.
2. Even when you agree with the price, sell long-dated positions: the break-even horizon is about 3 months at a 1¢ spread.
3. Always benchmark binary returns against T-bills over the same holding period.
