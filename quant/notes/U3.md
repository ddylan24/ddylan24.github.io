# U3: Partial pooling of calibration slopes (empirical Bayes)

**Problem.** Per-category calibration estimates are noisy: Polymarket "other" b = 0.51, Kalshi weather by city, and so on. Should each group get its own recalibration, one shared correction, or something in between?

## Model (`code/u3_hier_calib.py`)
- Per group: b̂_g ~ N(b_g, s_g²), with s_g the event-clustered SE of the logit calibration slope.
- Population: b_g ~ N(μ, τ²). τ² is estimated by DerSimonian–Laird method of moments: τ² = max(0, (Q − (k−1)) / (Σw − Σw²/Σw)), with w = 1/s² and Q Cochran's heterogeneity statistic.
- Posterior mean: **b̃_g = μ̂ + τ²/(τ² + s_g²) · (b̂_g − μ̂)**. This is James–Stein in meta-analysis clothing (see the earlier shrinkage module).

## Kalshi weather, six cities (Aug–Oct 2026)
| city | raw b ± se | shrunk |
|---|---|---|
| AUS | 1.21 ± 0.17 | 1.17 |
| CHI | 1.11 ± 0.18 | 1.17 |
| DEN | 1.32 ± 0.18 | 1.17 |
| LAX | 1.04 ± 0.16 | 1.17 |
| MIA | 1.04 ± 0.14 | 1.17 |
| NY | **1.47 ± 0.20** | 1.17 |

- Pooled μ̂ = 1.17. **τ̂ = 0**: Cochran Q = 4.7 on 5 df, completely consistent with no real difference between cities. So the estimator pools fully.
- NY's 1.47 vs Miami's 1.04 *looks* like a 0.4 difference but is noise.
- Out of sample (estimate on the first half of days, predict second-half city slopes): MSE raw 0.115, shrunk/pooled 0.119. Effectively a tie with 6 groups and about 30 days each, so there's no evidence that city-specific corrections help.

## Lessons
1. Before tailoring a correction per group, test heterogeneity (Cochran Q / τ²). If τ ≈ 0, use one pooled correction.
2. Partial pooling is the principled middle ground: weight = τ²/(τ² + s_g²). Noisy groups borrow strength from the rest.
3. The weather favorite-longshot slope (≈1.17, under-confident) is a *venue-level* feature here, not a city-level one.
