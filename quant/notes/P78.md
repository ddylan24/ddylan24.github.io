# P7 — Calibration vs horizon (Polymarket, scheduled-end design, 1¢<p<99¢)
Logit calibration: logit P(YES) = a + b·logit(price). b=1, a=0 is perfect; b<1 ⇒ prices too extreme (longshots underpriced, favorites overpriced); b>1 ⇒ prices pulled toward 50% (Page & Clemen FLB).
1d: b .87 (se .09), a +.19, n 620/383 events; 7d: .84 (.08), +.21; 30d: .83 (.08), +.16; 90d: .76 (.08), −.17; 180d: .84 (.11), +.06 (event-clustered SEs).
→ b ≈ 0.8 (≈2 se below 1) at all horizons: mild REVERSE favorite–longshot pattern (longshots a bit underpriced, favorites a bit overpriced), plus positive intercept (YES underpriced) at short horizons. Opposite direction to Page & Clemen's older-market finding. Hypothesis: popular "buy NO on longshots at 97–99¢" yield trade on Polymarket pushes longshot YES prices below fair. Tentative (2σ, one venue, top-volume sample).
