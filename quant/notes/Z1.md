# Z1: Net LIP economics by series (7 days, fills marked to settlement)

**Method (`code/z1_lip_net.py`).**
- For 8 series, every market that carried LIP rewards and settled in the last 7 days (up to ~400 per series, sampled evenly).
- **Pool** = all LIP rewards on those markets (full program history).
- **Maker fill P&L** = all public fills marked to settlement (Y1 method).
- Net = pool + fill P&L, i.e. what *all* makers together earned on those markets.

## Interpretation
- **In aggregate, LIP makers were paid about $256k and lost nothing net on fills** in these series over the week. Fills are roughly zero-mean (Y1), so the reward is the profit.
- **Reward intensity varies 6×.** Hourly temperature pays **5.8¢ per filled contract** of reward. FX 15m pays only ~1¢, because huge fill volume dilutes the pool.
- **Adverse selection is worst in state gas prices** (−3.3¢/contract, eating 92% of the pool) and next worst in Trump approval, AUD and crypto 15m (−0.9 to −1.2¢).
- **Ranking for a new LP** (reward intensity minus typical fill loss, robust-looking): **KXTEMPMIAH ≫ KXTRUMPAPPROVE > FX 15m ≈ crypto 15m > gas**. Rotten Tomatoes looks great but rests on too few markets to judge.
- **Caveats:**
  - One week.
  - Sampled markets.
  - Fills include all makers, not only LIP participants.
  - The "net" belongs to all qualifying LPs, split by score share (a newcomer ~20–35%, W1/LIP).
  - Settlement-marked P&L is noisy for series with few, lumpy outcomes. Use per-contract figures and longer windows (home PC).
