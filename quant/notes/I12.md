# I1 — Fundamental Law of Active Management (Grinold 1989; Grinold & Kahn)
IR ≈ IC · √BR (BR = independent bets/yr). Sim (independent assets, monthly, 120m, 200 runs): N=30 IC .05 → IR .94 vs .95; N=300 IC .05 → 3.00 vs 3.00; N=3000 IC .02 → 3.76 vs 3.79; N=300 IC .02 → 1.19 vs 1.20 ✓.
Real: industry momentum (12-1) on 30 KF industries, 1189 months: mean rank IC .067, IC std .313 → ICIR .74/yr; realized rank-weighted L/S Sharpe .49; naive FLAM .067·√360 = 1.27. Gap: (a) IC is noisy (time-varying) — effective IR ≈ mean(IC)/std(IC)·√12; (b) industries correlated → breadth ≪ N (participation ratio of correlation eigenvalues ≈ 2.3, dominated by market mode). Use ICIR, not IC·√N, for real signals.
# I2 — Factor regressions (Ken French FF5 + MOM monthly; HAC t-stats, 6 lags)
- AQR TSMOM 1985–2026: alpha +9.73%/yr (t 5.27), R² .17, MOM β .32 (t 6.6). 2010–2026: alpha +3.71% (t 1.36) — no longer significant; MOM β .49.
- Industry momentum L/S 1963–2026 vs FF5+MOM: alpha +0.79% (t 1.47), MOM β .32 (t 23.7), R² .59 → fully explained by stock momentum. Vs FF5 only: alpha +3.59% (t 4.37) → "alpha" depends on which factors you control for.
Lesson: before claiming alpha, regress on the known factors; leaving one out manufactures alpha.
