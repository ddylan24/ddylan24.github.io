# L3 — Currency carry & UIP (FRED: daily FX DEX*, OECD 3m interbank IR3TIB01*M156N; monthly 2002-04 → 2025-12; 9 currencies vs USD)
Excess return of foreign deposit: (S_{t+1}/S_t)(1+i_f/12) − (1+i_us/12), S = USD per FX. Signal = rate differential, lagged 1 month.
Long top-3 / short bottom-3: 2.5%/yr, vol 7.2%, Sharpe .35, skew −.63, worst month −10.6% (2008-09), max DD 30%. 2002–07 +45.5% (SR 1.08); 2008 −26.5%; 2009–26 +59.5% (SR .45).
Decomposition: rate differential +3.1%/yr, spot −0.6%/yr → high-yield currencies did NOT depreciate by the differential.
Fama (1984) pooled regression Δs_{t+1} on (i_f − i_us)/12: β = −0.13 (t −.35). UIP predicts −1; random walk 0 → forward-premium puzzle (data closer to random walk).
corr(carry, same-month ΔVIX) = −.46 → carry is short crash/volatility risk ("up the stairs, down the elevator"; Brunnermeier, Nagel & Pedersen 2008; Lustig & Verdelhan 2007; Koijen et al. 2018 "Carry").
