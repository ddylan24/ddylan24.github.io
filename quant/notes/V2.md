# V2: The BTC variance risk premium, and why it may explain the U4 candidate

**Definition.** VRP = implied vol − subsequently realized vol. Implied: Deribit **DVOL** (30-day BTC implied vol index). Realized: next-30-day vol of daily BTC-PERPETUAL closes, annualized with √365. Daily bars were re-stamped to the close (R1 lesson). Script: `code/v2_btc_vrp.py`.

## Results (2021-04 → 2026-09, 1,994 days)
- Mean DVOL 59.9 vs forward RV 50.9, so **VRP +9.0 vol points**. Implied exceeded realized on 74% of days.
- Non-overlapping 30-day windows: n = 67, mean **+9.5 ± 1.7 (t 5.6)**. Worst window **−24.0**: selling vol has crash risk.
- By year:

| year | DVOL | fwd RV | VRP | share > 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 91.9 | 73.1 | +18.8 | 86% |
| 2022 | 73.6 | 60.9 | +12.7 | 76% |
| 2023 | 49.6 | 42.8 | +6.8 | 71% |
| 2024 | 57.7 | 48.9 | +8.8 | 77% |
| 2025 | 46.0 | 41.7 | +4.4 | 70% |
| 2026 | 43.4 | 40.4 | **+3.0** | 63% |

- **Decaying premium.** It fell from 19 to 3 vol points as the market matured, the same arc as crypto funding carry (H: 19 → 1 point over T-bills). Harvested premia shrink as capital arrives.
- Mincer–Zarnowitz: RV = 9.7 + 0.68·DVOL (R² 0.48). DVOL is informative but biased upward and over-reactive: high-DVOL periods overstate the vol that follows even more.

## Link to prediction markets (hypothesis)
- T3: Polymarket and Kalshi BTC threshold prices imply vols matching options (34–40%, within 0–3 points).
- If the options vol overstates realized vol (VRP > 0), the venues' implied distributions are **too wide**. Every probability is pulled toward 50%, which is under-confidence.
- Under a lognormal, the right correction in probit space is a slope of σ_implied/σ_realized. 2026 annual numbers give 43.4/40.4 ≈ **1.07**. Short-dated vol premia are often larger, and U4 fitted ≈1.2 at 6h.
- So the U4 candidate edge is plausibly **the variance risk premium showing up in binary prices**. Selling over-wide binaries is short vol, with the same crash risk (worst window −24 vol points). Size it like a short-variance position.
- Testable: if this is right, the U4 slope should co-move with the short-dated VRP over time. Add to the home-PC queue.

## Lessons
1. Implied ≠ expected. Option-implied (and binary-implied) vols carry a premium for bearing variance risk.
2. Premia that people can harvest decay. Measure them by year, not just on average.
