# P2 — Polymarket calibration study (real data)
Data: Polymarket gamma API (closed markets ordered by volume; API paging stopped at ~2,100) + CLOB /prices-history (daily, fidelity=1440). Filter: binary Yes/No, cleanly resolved (outcomePrices 1/0), volume ≥ $50k → 1,751 markets, 1,621 with history; 2023-03 → 2026-10. YES won 23.3%.
Price = last daily price (not mid; spreads/fees ignored).
## Pitfall found: measuring "h days before CLOSE" leaks the outcome
"Will X happen by date" markets that resolve YES close EARLY; conditioning on close time selects outcomes. First pass (h days before closedTime): mid-priced (5–95¢) outcome − price = +.047 (1d), +.089 (7d, t 6.3), +.056 (30d). Corrected design: h days before SCHEDULED endDate, only markets still open then.
## Corrected results (scheduled end date)
1d: n=1,057 in 527 events, Brier .0995 vs base-rate .1938 (skill 49%). 7d: n=982, Brier .1054 vs .1908 (45%). 30d: n=946, Brier .0984 vs .1661 (41%).
Mid-priced (5–95¢) mean(outcome − price), event-clustered t: 1d +.041 (t 3.47, n 510/337 events); 7d +.053 (t 3.71); 30d +.047 (t 3.33).
Longshots (<10¢): 1d avg price .012 vs win .021; 7d .016 vs .033; 30d .018 vs .030 (a few wins drive this — small numbers).
Favorites (>90¢): 1d .968 vs .979; 7d .963 vs .941; 30d .944 vs .950.
By volume tercile (7d, close-based run): low-vol events +.122, mid +.095, high +.050 — bias largest in thinner markets.
## Interpretation & caveats
Prices are informative (41–49% Brier skill) but in this sample YES was underpriced by ~4–5¢ in mid-range markets (t≈3.5 clustered). Possible causes: NO-side demand ("nothing happens" bets / yield-like favorites), capital lockup, thin liquidity. Caveats: one venue, ~3.5 years, top-volume selection (only ~2,100 markets returned), last-trade prices, no fees/spread, outcomes clustered within events (used event-clustered SE). Not a tradable result until tested out-of-sample with bid/ask and fees.
