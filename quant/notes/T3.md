# T3: Term structure of BTC distributions across Polymarket and Kalshi

**Question.** Q3 compared single contracts. Here I fit a whole lognormal (center F, total variance w = σ²T) to each venue-expiry's threshold mids and check two things:
1. Are the term structures consistent with each other and with Deribit?
2. Is there calendar arbitrage? Total variance must not fall with T across expiries of the same underlying.

## Data and method (`code/t3_term.py`)
- Live snapshot from `code/q3_digitals.py --smooth` (2026-10-14 ~23:40 UTC).
- Least-squares fit of P(S>K) = N((ln F/K − w/2)/√w) to mids with 3–97% probability. Polymarket strikes are converted from USDT to USD.
- The same fit applied to the Deribit smile digitals at the same strikes.

- **No calendar arbitrage.** Total variance rises monotonically through all 8 interleaved expiries of the two venues (3.9 → 23.4 ×10⁻⁴).
- **Vol term structures agree with options within 0–3 vol points.** Both show the weekend dip (Oct 18, Saturday: 30–31%) that's typical of crypto. The venues price the calendar of activity too.
- **Centers stay flat near spot (≈$81.5k) while the options forward rises with carry** (81.59k → 81.75k). The gap grows from about $100 to about $190 with maturity. This confirms Q3's "PM traders center on spot, not the forward" across the whole curve.
- A single lognormal fits each venue's strikes to 0.6–1.7¢ RMSE, about one spread. Skew adds a little beyond that (Q3).

## Lessons
1. Check total-variance monotonicity across *all* venues on the same underlying. It's a cheap screen for calendar arbitrage (none found here).
2. Cross-venue BTC binaries form one coherent surface, with a single systematic difference: centering on spot instead of the forward (the funding carry).
