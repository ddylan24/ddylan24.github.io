Questions

The things owners ask first

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site is yours, the domain stays in your name, and your customer list is your data. If you ever leave, we hand it over — we don't hold your business hostage.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?

Usually we replace it, because the booking and dashboard need to be wired in properly. If your current site is working fine and you just want the booking and owner dashboard, we can talk about that too.

Do I have to be technical?

No. You never log into a hosting panel or update a plugin. If you want a price changed or a class added, you text us and it's done — or you change it yourself from your dashboard, whichever you prefer.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract. The monthly covers hosting, maintenance and support, and you can stop any time. We'd rather keep you because the site is making you money.

How long until I'm live?

Usually about a week from our first conversation. Most of that is you reviewing what we built and telling us what to fix.

Can I take deposits or payments online?

Deposits at booking are built in. If you want full card payments, we connect Stripe so money goes straight to your account — we never touch it.

Let's see if this fits your business

Fifteen minutes on the phone, no pressure and no pitch deck. If it's not a fit we'll tell you straight — and you'll still get a couple of ideas worth having.
